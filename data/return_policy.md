# Return and Refund Policy (demo company "KartNova")

## Return window
A claim must be raised within a limited number of days after delivery. Days allowed for each product category:
- electronics: 7 days
- clothing: 10 days
- footwear: 10 days
- glassware: 5 days
Claims raised after the return window has passed are not eligible for replacement or refund.

## Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, the customer must upload at least one clear photo that shows the damage. The photo must show the same product that was ordered. Eligible damage claims receive a replacement or a full refund. The damaged item is collected by the delivery partner when the replacement is handed over.

## Auto-approval limits
Damage claims with very high verification confidence may be approved automatically, but only when the order value is up to 5000 rupees. Orders above 5000 rupees always require a pickup with inspection, even when the photo looks convincing.

## Pickup with inspection checklist
When a pickup is required the delivery partner must follow this checklist before handing over the replacement:
1. Compare the item with the listing photo and confirm it is the same product.
2. Photograph the damage on the item itself, including the packaging and the shipping label.
3. Check that the damage is physical and not cosmetic wear caused by the customer.
4. Collect the damaged item and record the pickup in the app.

## Fraud and duplicate evidence
A photo that matches a photo from another customer's claim is escalated to a human reviewer. A claim is never rejected on suspicion alone: suspicious claims always go to a human reviewer. Customers with 3 or more claims in the last 90 days are also reviewed by a human.

## Non-returnable items
Customised or personalised products, gift cards and opened hygiene items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.
